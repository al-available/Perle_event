OUR STORY
Born from Passion,
Driven by Love
In the vibrant heart of Addis Ababa, Perle Event & More was born from a simple belief: that every moment of celebration deserves to be wrapped in beauty and love.
What began as a passion for creating stunning gift presentations has grown into a full-service event styling and creative gifting company. We pour our hearts into every ribbon we tie, every balloon we inflate, and every bouquet we arrange.
Based in Tseddo, we serve clients across Addis Ababa and beyond — turning ordinary occasions into extraordinary memories that families cherish for a lifetime.
